# M01 — MiniCPM5-1B LoRA merge-equivalence validation

## Hypothesis

The direct merger produces the exact weighted sum of the independently trained
math and formatting LoRA deltas on the common `openbmb/MiniCPM5-1B` base. The
observed PEFT `linear` collapse is a composition-method failure, not evidence
that PEFT merging or MiniCPM projection weights are intrinsically corrupt.

## Method

Audit adapter topology and scaling, then compare direct-merged parameter
tensors and greedy FP16 logits against: (1) both PEFT adapters active together,
(2) native PEFT merge of both active adapters, and (3) weighted PEFT `cat` and
`linear` combinations where supported. Repeat deterministic behavioral checks
on the existing Q4 GGUF exports. This is an implementation-validation pilot:
there is no stochastic training, so three training seeds are not applicable.

## Seeds

Pilot — deterministic FP16 and Q4 inference; fixed prompt set and greedy
decoding. A later training-effect replication will use seeds 42, 137, and 2026.

## Metrics

- Adapter key/pair and target-module coverage.
- Maximum and mean absolute parameter difference from the analytic delta sum.
- Maximum logit difference and greedy-token agreement on fixed probes.
- Q4 greedy output equality/behavioral regression relative to FP16.
- Paired GSM8K and JSON outcomes, with bootstrap confidence intervals.

## Expected result

Direct merge and simultaneous PEFT adapters agree within FP16 numerical error;
`cat` agrees if supported. `linear` is allowed to differ materially because it
factor-combines adapters rather than necessarily summing their deltas.

## Falsification criteria

Reject the direct-merger validity claim if any targeted tensor differs from the
analytic sum by more than 1e-3, if greedy FP16 outputs disagree across correct
sum implementations, or if an adapter's keys are silently excluded.

## Estimated compute time

20 minutes CPU/one 8GB GPU for FP16 checks; existing Q4 outputs are reused.

## Dependencies

Existing `minicpm5_1b_math_adapter`, `minicpm5_1b_format_adapter`, and direct
merge artifacts from 2026-07-09.
